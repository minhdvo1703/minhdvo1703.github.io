Who I am

About Me

Hello! My name is Minh — you can also call me Claire.

Originally from Vietnam, I earned my Bachelor's in Business Analytics from the University of South Florida. During my undergraduate studies, I completed three internships in business intelligence, sales operations, and data analytics, sharpening my skills in SQL, Python, and Tableau to turn large datasets into business insights.

Those experiences revealed how transformative data can be across industries, which drove me to pursue a Master's in Applied Data Science at the University of Chicago. There I deepened my expertise in machine learning, deep learning, and scalable data engineering to tackle complex, interdisciplinary problems.

My passion is to harness data-driven insights for informed decision-making — optimizing processes, uncovering opportunities, and bridging the gap between data, technology, and business impact.
